  1. Load the filament: heat the nozzle, cut the filament tip at an angle, push it in, and press Load until plastic comes out.裝線材:先加熱噴嘴,把線材前端斜剪,插入進料口,按「進料」直到噴嘴擠出塑膠。
  2. Drag both files into the slicer and stand them up like the picture.把兩個檔案拖進切片軟體,照圖片的方式立起來。
  3. Pick the settings in the table, then Slice and Print.照表格選好設定,然後按「切片」再按「列印」。
  4. Watch the first few minutes to make sure it sticks.前幾分鐘要看著,確認有黏在平台上。
  5. Let it cool, take the parts off and peel away the brim.等它冷卻,取下零件,撕掉裙邊。
  6. Push the nose cone into the tube. If it is tight, sand the bottom part of the nose cone.把鼻錐插進管子。太緊的話,用砂紙磨鼻錐底部。

2. Pack it2. 組裝

  1. Press 10 g of modelling clay (a marble-sized ball) into the tip of the nose cone. Without it the rocket tumbles.把 10 克黏土(約一顆彈珠大)壓進鼻錐尖端。沒有它火箭會翻滾。
  2. Tie about 50 cm of elastic or Kevlar cord to the bar inside the nose cone. Glue the other end inside the tube, above the motor mount.把約 50 公分的彈性繩或克維拉繩綁在鼻錐裡的橫桿上,另一端黏在管子內側、發動機座上方。
  3. Tie a streamer to the cord near the nose cone.在靠近鼻錐的繩子上綁一條飄帶。
  4. Push 3 or 4 sheets of recovery wadding into the tube from the top, then the streamer and cord, then the nose cone.從管口塞入 3 到 4 張防火隔熱紙,再放飄帶和繩子,最後蓋上鼻錐。
  5. Wrap tape around an 18 mm motor (B or C size) until it pushes in snugly from the back. It sticks out about 6 mm.在 18 mm 發動機(B 或 C 級)外面纏膠帶,直到從尾端塞入時剛好緊。它會露出約 6 mm。
  6. Balance the rocket on one finger. It should balance 19 cm or less from the nose tip. If the balance point is further back, add more clay.用一根手指平衡火箭。平衡點應該在距離鼻錐尖端 19 公分以內。如果太靠後,就再加黏土。

3. Launch it3. 發射

  1. Pick a big open field, away from dry grass, trees and power lines.選一個空曠的大場地,遠離乾草、樹木和電線。
  2. Put the igniter and plug into the motor, following the motor's instructions.依照發動機的說明,裝上點火器和塞子。
  3. Slide the rocket onto the launch rod.把火箭套上發射桿。
  4. Clip the controller wires to the igniter.把控制器的夾子夾在點火器上。
  5. Walk back at least 5 m, count down and press the button.退後至少 5 公尺,倒數,按下按鈕。

Checklist before launch發射前檢查表

The motor burns real fuel. Follow the instructions on the motor package and keep your face and hands away from the back of the rocket once the igniter is in.發動機燃燒的是真正的燃料。請依照包裝上的說明操作,裝上點火器後,臉和手都要遠離火箭尾端。
